# 🦉 Duolingo for CA (Circuit Analysis)

A gamified, bite-sized learning app designed to help Electrical & Electronic Engineering students master Ohm’s law, Kirchhoff’s Law, Thevenin and Norton circuits, Laplace circuits and AC/DC analysis.
---

## 📌 Features

**Micro-Lessons:** Break down heavy circuit analysis concepts into fast, 5-minute daily modules.
- **Interactive Exercise Types:**
  - **Component Matching:** Match circuit symbols to their physical components or schematic terms.
  - **Value Fill-in-the-Blanks:** Calculate unknown voltages, currents, or resistances ($V = IR$).
  - **Schematic Spot-the-Error:** Identify incorrectly wired loops or broken branch logic.
  - **Multiple Choice Questions:** Pick the correct answer out of the answers given.
- **Streaks & XP:** Build daily study habits, collect XP, and compete on engineering student leaderboards.
