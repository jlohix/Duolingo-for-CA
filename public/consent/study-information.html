Informed consent form

This page is the consent sheet students complete in Circuito. It follows the LeaDS informed consent form for students (non-HBR).

1. Taking part

By selecting the following, I agree that I have read the purpose of the study and this consent form.

I voluntarily consent to take part in this research study. I have fully read, discussed, and understood the purpose and procedures of this study as stated in the Study Information Sheet attached to this consent form. My questions concerning the study have been answered to my satisfaction, and I acknowledge that I am participating in this study of my own free will.

I understand that I may withdraw my consent and stop participating in the study at any time without giving any reasons, and without penalty.

By participating in this research study, I confirm that I consent to the collection, use and disclosure of my Personal Data for the purposes set out in the Study Information Sheet.

I agree that I will be contacted for additional consent, including but not limited to changes in the proposed research, serious adverse events that would lead to a change in the proposed research, and any other circumstances which is specific to this research study.

I have read and understood the above consent form. I indicate my willingness to voluntarily take part in the study. If you choose not to take part in this study, you can still use the chatbot; however, your data will not be used for research purposes to improve this and other chatbots deployed in NTU.

2. Consent for the use of data for future research

YES: I agree to have my identifiable data stored for future research with the understanding that this would only be done upon approval from the Institutional Review Board or local ethics committee.

NO: I do not agree to donate my identifiable data for future research.

3. If yes, please check one

There are no restrictions on the kind of research that may be done with my data.

The Investigator may use my data for future research as long as the research is related to Project NALA / NTU Education Transformation.

4. Consent to be contacted for future research opportunities

YES: I agree to be contacted for future research that I may be eligible for.

NO: I do not agree to be contacted for future research.
